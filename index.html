Accommodations

Rest in Refined Comfort

Nestled along the serene banks of the historic Kandalama Tank in Dambulla, Gaia Lake Bungalow offers an exquisite blend of modern luxury and raw natural beauty. This private 4-bedroom sanctuary provides a peaceful countryside retreat with breathtaking views of the water and the dramatic mountain ranges in the background. Whether you are watching the sunrise from our rustic wooden deck, taking a dip in the private swimming pool, or enjoying a meal prepared by our in-house chef, this villa promises full privacy and complete tranquility.

🏡 The Villa At A Glance

  • Capacity: 4 Bedrooms | 4 Bathrooms | Sleep up to 8-10 guests comfortably.
  • Outdoor Oasis: Sprawling tropical gardens rolling down to the lake, a child-friendly swimming pool, sun terrace, and an outdoor fireplace.
  • Living & Dining: Fully equipped kitchen (with oven, microwave, refrigerator), a spacious shared lounge, and an outdoor dining area perfect for starry nights.
  • Complimentary Perks: Free high-speed Wi-Fi, private parking, and free bicycles to explore nearby trails.
